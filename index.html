How To Play

Goal: Collect more coins than your rivals before time runs out. Watch for random events that shake up the arena!

Move: WASD or Arrow Keys (keyboard) · Virtual Joystick (touch)

Dash: SPACE or DASH button — short burst of speed, brief hazard immunity.

Ability: E or ability button — Coin Pulse, pulls nearby coins toward you.

Power-Ups: Speed Boost, Shield, Magnet, Double Score, Dash Boost, Coin Multiplier, Extra Dash Charge, Slow Opponents.

Hazards: Traps slow you down. Jump Pads launch you forward. Moving zones drift around the arena.

Final 15 seconds trigger FINAL RUSH — extra coins, extra chaos.
